### ★ Insight ─────────────────────────────────────
This ES module migration issue is common when upgrading Node.js projects from CommonJS to ES modules. The `__dirname` global is specific to CommonJS, while ES modules require explicit path resolution using `import.meta.url`. Production deployments often catch these issues that work in development due to transpilation differences.
